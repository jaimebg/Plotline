## Description

```
Plotline tells you whether a series is worth your time, and shows its working.

Most apps hand you a rating. Plotline analyses every episode of a series and tells you what the numbers actually say — then prints the numbers underneath, so you can disagree with it.

WHAT PLOTLINE WORKS OUT

• Plotline Score — our own 0-100 rating, broken into the three things it measures: how highly the episodes rate, how evenly the show holds that level, and whether it climbs or slides.

• Where it falls off — the season after which quality drops and never recovers, with the averages before and after.

• How steady it is — from remarkably even to a genuine rollercoaster, measured between its highest and lowest rated episodes.

• Whether it hooks you early — or starts slow and gets good later, with the season it turns around.

• Whether it lands the ending — ends on a high, holds its level, or fades out.

• Standout episodes — the ones far above their own season, and the ones far below. Judged within each season, so a high point of a weaker year still shows up.

• Episode-by-episode charts across every season.

SHELVES YOU WON'T FIND ELSEWHERE

Shows That Never Slip. Knows When It Peaked. Worth the Wait. They Stick the Landing. Brilliant and Baffling. These aren't genres — they're groups we derived by analysing every episode of the series we ship, each with the measurement behind it spelled out.

WORKS BEFORE YOU DO ANYTHING

122 fully analysed series ship inside the app. No account, no sign-up, no subscription. Open it on a plane and the scores, verdicts and standout episodes are all there.

ALSO INSIDE

• Where to watch, by streaming service, with a region selector
• Compare any two titles side by side
• Career profiles for any actor or director
• Trend explorers: genre evolution, best years, decade battles, franchise tracking
• Favorites and a watchlist, synced across your devices
• Full support for iPhone and iPad, light and dark

WHEN IT WON'T GUESS

If a series has too few rated episodes to support a conclusion, Plotline says so and tells you why, instead of inventing a verdict. We would rather say nothing than say something we cannot back up.

Metadata and ratings from TMDB. Streaming data provided by JustWatch. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
```

## A note on the required attributions

Two are not optional and both must appear where the data is shown:

- **TMDB**: "This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB." Included at the end of the description above. The TMDB logo requirement is handled in-app.
- **JustWatch**: TMDB's terms for the watch-providers endpoint require crediting JustWatch as the source and state that non-compliance revokes API access. The app draws that credit in the same view as the providers; the description above also names them.
